1. How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
I interpret user needs by viewing the application through the eyes of the traveler. Creating User Stories is essential because it provides a clear "Definition of Done." For example, a story like "As a traveler, I want to filter flight results by price" ensures the development and testing phases are guided by user value rather than just a technical checklist.

2. How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
I approach development using a sprint-based iterative approach. This allows for a continuous feedback loop and the ability to pivot when requirements change. In the future, I hope to incorporate Daily Stand-ups to identify blockers in real-time and Sprint Reviews to maintain transparency with stakeholders and ensure the project stays aligned with user needs.

3. What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
Being a good team member means understanding and respecting Scrum roles. It involves communicating clearly during Daily Stand-ups, being honest about technical debt or blockers, and collaborating with the Product Owner to ensure the team meets the Sprint Goal without scope creep.
